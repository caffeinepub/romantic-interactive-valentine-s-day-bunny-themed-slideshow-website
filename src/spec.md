# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Convert the Valentine slideshow into an 11-page “cute boy with glasses” love story, updating the intro, interactions, visuals, and final greeting while keeping transitions smooth and music autoplay gating intact.

**Planned changes:**
- Restructure the slideshow flow to exactly 11 pages (plus a pre-intro overlay) with the requested page order and navigation behavior.
- Update the pre-intro overlay to a “Happy Valentine Day love with hearts” themed animation while preserving reduced-motion behavior.
- Update Page 1 copy to exactly “HEY JAAN ❤️” with a Continue button.
- Keep Page 2 as a fade-in message page with the exact provided sentence and a Continue button.
- Update Page 3 Valentine question behavior: first NO shows “Wrong answer! Try again 😤” with an angry boy-with-glasses animation then returns to the question; second NO fades out and disables; YES triggers a hearts+stars+moon confetti overlay before advancing.
- Update Pages 4, 6, 7, and 9 to use boy-with-glasses visuals and the exact provided text/buttons; add/keep specified effects (sparkles, heart particles, floating hearts).
- Keep Page 5 as a 6-card love letter gallery; each letter includes a pasteable photo placeholder, the specified caption order, and an editable notes box; include “Continue ❤️”.
- Keep Page 8 as the moonrise envelope opening sequence with an editable letter and Continue button.
- Add Page 10 as a full animated story sequence: long-distance → love → marriage → family with 5 children, mobile-friendly and respecting reduced-motion (no auto-advance when reduced motion is enabled).
- Add Page 11 as the closing screen with exact text “Happy Valentine Day my love my future wifey” and an editable final note box.
- Remove remaining bunny/panda references from user-facing copy and alt text across updated pages to standardize on the boy-with-glasses theme.

**User-visible outcome:** Users experience an 11-page Valentine slideshow with a cute boy-with-glasses theme, updated interactions (YES/NO logic, proposal choices), editable message/note areas, a new animated story page, and a final greeting page.
